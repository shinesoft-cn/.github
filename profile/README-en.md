## 👋 Who We Are

We are Guangzhou ShineSoft, the team behind **Sublyric**——
an AI-driven subtitle creation tool covering the full workflow from
**speech transcription → translation → fine editing → karaoke effects → final export**,
built for both professional editors and everyday users.

- 🎙️ AI transcription, automatic timeline alignment, translation in 19+ languages
- 🎬 Multi-track subtitles synced with audio waveform, CPS control, millisecond-precision adjustment
- 🎤 50+ dynamic karaoke effect presets, K-Cut intelligent syllable splitting
- 📤 Supports MP4/MKV/MOV video formats and exports to SRT/ASS/WebVTT/Word/Excel and 10+ other formats

👉 [Download for free](https://www.shinesoft.cn/en/download/) | [Tutorials & Case Studies](https://www.shinesoft.cn/en/case-study/)
